Problem & Solution

Why parking fails students

The Problem

  • No system to predict when a zone fills up
  • 8 AM students compete equally with 2 PM students for nearby spots
  • No real-time feedback before entering campus
  • Remote zones underutilised while nearby zones overflow
  • Time lost searching adds stress before class begins

The Solution

  • Connect student timetable to parking allocation
  • Upcoming-class students get priority for nearby zones
  • Live availability visible before arriving on campus
  • Session ends on vehicle exit, not class end
  • Remote zones always open, no reservation needed

Mini Research

Parking congestion at universities is a well-documented challenge, particularly during morning peak hours when multiple faculties start simultaneously. Schedule-aware systems that allocate limited resources based on urgency are an established approach in transportation management. At UoS, multiple zones exist at varying distances from faculties — a priority system can distribute demand intelligently across these zones without requiring new physical infrastructure.

Why It Matters

Universities already collect student schedule data. Parkly bridges schedule data with zone occupancy data to make smarter allocation decisions automatically — improving the daily experience of thousands of students without adding new parking capacity.

Core Logic

Three rules.
Smarter parking.

RULE 01
Schedule = Priority
When parking is scarce, students with an upcoming class get priority for nearby zones. No class soon? You still park — just further out. Fair, automatic, no disputes.
RULE 02
Real-Time Occupancy = Availability
Live zone data is calculated from sensors and active sessions. You see exactly how many spots are open before entering campus — no guessing, no circling.
RULE 03
Vehicle Exit = Session End
Your session stays active while your car is parked. Class ending does not end your session. Unexpected meetings are fine. When you drive out, the spot opens automatically.

Priority only affects new parking allocations. A student already parked is never displaced. A 15-minute grace period applies before vehicle arrival. Remote parking is always available with no reservation required.

Process

How Parkly works

Five steps from timetable to parked car.

01

Connect Your Timetable

Parkly links to your university student portal to read your class schedule. Your data stays within the university system — no third-party sharing.

02

Check Real-Time Availability

On the morning of your class, open Parkly to see live zone occupancy across campus. Zones near your faculty are highlighted based on your schedule.

03

Get Priority When It Matters

If parking is limited, students with an upcoming class receive priority for nearby zones. No class soon? You can still park — in a zone with available space.

04

Reserve & Enter Automatically

Confirm your zone in the app. When you arrive, the gate reads your plate and opens automatically. Your session starts the moment you enter.

05

Leave Whenever You're Done

Drive out when you're ready — after class, after a meeting, whenever. The exit gate detects your vehicle and ends the session automatically.
